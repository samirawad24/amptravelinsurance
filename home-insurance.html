What home insurance can cover

  • Your home's structure

    Repairs or rebuilds your home after covered damage like fire or wind.

  • Your belongings

    Furniture, electronics, clothes, and other things you own.

  • Liability

    Helps if someone gets hurt at your home or you damage someone else's property.

  • Living expenses

    Helps pay for a place to stay if covered damage forces you out of your home.

  • Flood

    Standard home policies do not cover flood. You can buy a separate flood policy.

  • Rental property you own

    Landlord policies cover the building and your liability when you rent it out.

Coverage, limits, deductibles, and exclusions depend on the policy you choose.

Questions and answers

Does home insurance cover hurricanes?
Most Florida home policies cover wind damage from hurricanes, usually with a separate hurricane deductible. Flooding, including storm surge, is not covered without a flood policy.
What do you need to quote my home?
The property type, ZIP code, the year it was built if you know it, and the owner's name and date of birth. We will ask for the full address and other details in our chat.
I own a condo. What do I need?
A condo policy usually covers the inside of your unit, your belongings, and your liability. Your association's master policy covers the building's shared parts.
Can you quote a home I rent out?
Yes. Choose "Rental property I own" in the form.
