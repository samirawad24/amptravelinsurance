Questions and answers

Which types of insurance can you quote?
Auto, home and property, renters, travel, and business insurance. I do not offer life or health insurance.
How does a WhatsApp quote work?
Fill in the quote form or message me directly. I review what you need and send options in the chat. You ask questions, pick a policy if you want one, and I help with the next steps.
Is there any cost to get a quote?
No. Quotes and guidance are free with no obligation.
Are you my agent or my advisor?
For travel insurance, I am your agent. For auto, home, renters, and business insurance, I am your advisor: I gather your details and explain your options, and a licensed agent at AGB Insurance prepares your quote and issues your policy. You keep talking to me the whole time.
Do you help in Spanish?
Yes. I help you fully in English and Spanish.
What information should I not send?
Please do not send driver's license numbers, VINs, Social Security numbers, health details, passport numbers, or payment information through the form. If a quote needs any of these, I will ask for it in our chat.
